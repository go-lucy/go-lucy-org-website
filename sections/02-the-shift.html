02 / The shift

Software development as we once knew it is changing.

AI has moved the bottleneck from writing code to judging it.

Yet we’re still organizing teams, workflows, and processes as though writing code were the scarce resource.

The big players in AI have written frameworks and playbooks for how to utilize AI to build faster and higher quality, and the market is flooded with AI vendors. These resources are a strong start, but what is missing is the practical layer:

  • What is realistic for my organization, given its size and structure?
  • How do I get there, and what has to change along the way?
  • Which tools and playbooks do I use, and when?

The Go LUCY movement is an AI-era, community-driven, vendor-agnostic process for software development.
