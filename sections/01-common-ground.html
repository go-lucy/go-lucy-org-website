01 / Common ground

The Agile Manifesto came out of a three-day meeting in Utah in February 2001. Seventeen practitioners attended, each the champion of a different lightweight method for software development. The point was to find common ground across rival methods rather than crown one.

In this era, the people who needed software could not build it. We split the work across roles and frameworks so a specialist team could produce it for everyone else.

But the constraint that justified this entire system is collapsing.
